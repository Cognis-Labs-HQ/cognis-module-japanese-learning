# Generated Vocabulary Illustrations

Original AI-generated educational illustrations created for Cognis with OpenAI image generation. Japanese artwork uses an anime style. No third-party photographs are included.

The illustrations are bundled under the module's AGPL-3.0-or-later license. The [catalog](catalog.json) records the generation source, style, source-sheet or standalone-source hashes, exact crop coordinates, JPEG checksums and card assignments.
