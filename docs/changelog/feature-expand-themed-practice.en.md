# Themed Practice

**Feature Branch:** `feature-expand-themed-practice`

## Practice in context

Add four starter decks for food and drink, travel and direction, connecting ideas, and people and preferences. They provide focused practice with 23 existing phrases and their linked meanings, kana readings and constituents.

## Clearer Illustrations

Replace the illustrations for とても and しかし. A high-temperature scene conveys degree, while expected sunshine contrasted with actual rain conveys the contrasting relationship.

## Usage in Context

Clarify the illustrated senses of きれい, ゆっくり and あし with localized usage notes and examples. Give だけど a preceding statement that makes the contrast explicit.
